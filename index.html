About Patrick Gorman

20 years of getting supply chains to perform.

Patrick Gorman has spent more than two decades at the sharp end of global logistics, not in consulting, but running sales, building teams, and delivering commercial outcomes for some of the world's leading freight and supply chain businesses.

His career spans international freight forwarding with Röhlig Logistics across Australia and the United States, national sales leadership at Visy Logistics where he managed a $350M supply chain operation, nearly a decade as National Product Manager at C.H. Robinson, one of the world's largest third-party logistics providers, and most recently as Director of Strategic Sales at Scan Global Logistics Australia.

Across those roles, Patrick has worked across road, rail, air, sea and coastal shipping; built and led national sales teams; developed coastal shipping divisions and warehouse networks; and driven commercial strategy across FMCG, retail and manufacturing sectors in Australia, Asia, Europe and the US.

Gorman Advisory was founded to make that experience available directly to businesses who need practical, commercially grounded advice, without the overhead of a large firm or the inexperience of a generalist consultant.
